Welcome to Riverbend Coffee Company. This handbook explains how our store runs day to day, what we expect from every team member, and what you can expect from us in return. Read it during your first week and keep it handy, because most of the questions new hires ask are answered here. If something is unclear or missing, ask your shift lead or the store manager, Dana Whitfield, who can be reached at the store phone, (936) 555-0142.

Store hours are 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on Saturday, and 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM on Sunday. The store is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day, and closes early at 2:00 PM on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve. Holiday hours are posted on the front door and on the break room board at least two weeks in advance.

Opening tasks begin 45 minutes before the doors open, so the opening shift starts at 5:15 AM on weekdays and 6:15 AM on weekends. The opener disarms the alarm, turns on the espresso machines and brewers so they can reach temperature, and starts the first batch of drip coffee. Next, they pull the day's pastries from the walk-in freezer to thaw, count the cash drawer to confirm it holds exactly $200, and check that the dining room, restrooms, and patio are clean and stocked. The opener signs the opening checklist on the clipboard by the back door before unlocking the front entrance.

Closing responsibilities start 30 minutes before the doors lock. The closing team stops brewing new drip coffee, backflushes and cleans both espresso machines, empties and sanitizes the milk pitchers, and wipes down every counter and table. All perishable food is labeled with the date and moved to the walk-in cooler, and anything past its date is logged on the waste sheet and thrown out. The closer counts the drawer, keeps $200 in it for the next morning, and places the remaining cash and the deposit slip in the office safe. Trash goes to the dumpster, the floors are swept and mopped, and the closing checklist is signed before the alarm is set.

Alarm procedures must be followed exactly every time. The alarm panel is just inside the back door, and the alarm code is 4827. To disarm the system when opening, enter 4827 followed by the OFF key within 30 seconds of opening the door. To arm it when closing, make sure everyone is out of the building, enter 4827 followed by the AWAY key, and leave through the back door within 60 seconds. If the alarm goes off by mistake, enter the code right away, then call the monitoring company at (800) 555-0199 and give the verbal passcode "bluebird" so they do not send police. Never share the alarm code with anyone who does not work here, and tell the manager immediately if you think it has been exposed.

Shift scheduling is done one week at a time. The schedule for the following week is posted every Thursday by 5:00 PM on the break room board and in the When I Work app. Submit your availability and any time-off requests in the app at least two weeks in advance; requests submitted later are approved only if coverage allows. If you want to trade a shift, you may arrange the swap with a coworker yourself, but both of you must confirm it in the app and the manager must approve it before it counts. Standard shifts are 6 or 8 hours, and no one is scheduled for more than 40 hours in a week without the manager's approval.

Attendance and call-outs matter because a short-staffed shift affects the whole team. If you are sick or cannot make it, call the store phone at least 2 hours before your shift starts; a text message alone is not enough. If you will be late, call as soon as you know. Three unexcused absences or five late arrivals within 90 days will lead to a written warning, and a no-call, no-show is treated as a voluntary resignation unless there was a genuine emergency.

Breaks and meals depend on how long you work. A shift of 6 hours or more includes one unpaid 30-minute meal break, and every 4 hours worked earns one paid 10-minute rest break. Coordinate breaks with your shift lead so the counter is never left with fewer than two people during busy periods. Team members get one free drink per shift and a 50 percent discount on food and drinks when they are working; the discount drops to 25 percent on days off.

Benefits are available to all employees, with more offered to full-time staff. Full-time team members, meaning those who average 30 or more hours per week, become eligible for medical, dental, and vision insurance on the first day of the month after 60 days of employment, and the company pays 70 percent of the employee-only premium. Paid time off accrues at 1 hour for every 30 hours worked for all employees, up to a maximum of 80 hours. After one year of service, employees may join the 401(k) plan, and the company matches 50 percent of contributions up to 4 percent of pay.

Uniform guidelines keep the team looking clean and consistent. Every team member wears a plain black or charcoal shirt with no logos or graphics, plus the green Riverbend apron provided on the first day. Pants must be black or dark denim with no rips, and shoes must be closed-toe and non-slip. Long hair must be tied back, and hats are allowed only if they are plain black or the company cap. Jewelry should be kept to a minimum for food safety, and nametags must be worn and visible at all times. You are responsible for keeping your apron clean; worn-out aprons can be swapped for a new one at no cost.

Food safety and cleanliness are everyone's job. Wash your hands for at least 20 seconds when you start a shift, after a break, after handling trash or money, and any time you switch tasks. Milk must stay at or below 41 degrees Fahrenheit, and any container left out of the cooler for more than 2 hours is thrown away. Sanitizer buckets are changed every 4 hours, and the test strip result is recorded on the log sheet. If you are sick with vomiting, diarrhea, or a fever, do not come to work; call in and follow the call-out procedure.

Pay and timekeeping are handled through the When I Work app. Clock in no more than 5 minutes before your scheduled start time and clock out as soon as your shift ends. Paychecks are issued every other Friday by direct deposit, and pay stubs are available in the payroll portal. Tips are pooled and shared every pay period among all non-management team members, based on hours worked. If you spot a mistake on your paycheck or timecard, tell the manager within 7 days so it can be fixed on the next check.
